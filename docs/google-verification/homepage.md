# Calinator

**A modern calendar for the terminal, with two-way sync.** Made for Omarchy, works on any Linux desktop.

![Calinator week view](screenshot-week.png)

## What it does

- Day, week, month and agenda views, with your calendars in your desktop's colors.
- **Two-way sync** with Google Calendar, iCloud and any CalDAV server (Fastmail, Nextcloud…). Create, change, move and delete events; changes from your phone show up within a minute.
- Repeating events done right: change *this event*, *this and following* or *all events*.
- Time zones and daylight saving time handled correctly.
- Answer invitations (yes / maybe / no). Calinator asks you before emailing guests.
- Reminders as desktop notifications, also when the app is closed.
- Works offline: changes wait and sync later. Nothing is lost when two places change the same event.
- Quick add in plain words: "lunch with Sarah tomorrow 1pm".

## Your data

Calinator runs on your computer. It has **no servers**: your calendar data goes only between your computer and your calendar service. Sign-ins and passwords are kept in your system keyring. No ads, no tracking.

Read the [privacy policy](PRIVACY_POLICY_URL).

## How Calinator uses your Google account

When you connect Google Calendar, Calinator asks to see and edit your calendar events and to see the list of your calendars. It uses this only to show your calendars in the app and to save the changes you make back to Google Calendar. Details are in the [privacy policy](PRIVACY_POLICY_URL).
